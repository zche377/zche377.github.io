I am a PhD student working with Dr. Michael F. Bonner, and I expect to finish in 2028. I am broadly interested in how visual systems, biological and artificial, come to represent the world the way they do. So far in my PhD, I have studied which parts of a visual representation are universal across these systems and which are idiosyncratic, how visual experience and learning objectives shape that split, and how much the answer depends on the measure used to compare them.

Prior to JHU, I received my bachelor's degree from Emory University. I worked as a research assistant with Dr. Daniel Dilks on low-level visual features relevant to human visual scene processing.
